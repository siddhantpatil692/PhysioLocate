# PhysioLocate


**About**

Find physiotherapy clinics near you — filtered by distance, insurance, reviews, and availability.

**The Story Behind PhysioLocate**

In the summer of 2026, I hurt my thumb fencing at York University. Then I hurt it again. The second time, it ended in a stress fracture.

Sitting in the hospital waiting room, I asked the doctor for a physiotherapy referral. They handed me a clinic that was over an hour away. For a thumb. I remember thinking — there is absolutely no way I am commuting two hours round trip so someone can get my thumb moving again.

That same night, lying in the hospital, I ordered Uber Eats to my room. And it hit me. Why is it easier to get Pad Thai delivered to a hospital bed than it is to find a physiotherapist ten minutes from your house?

PhysioLocate is my answer to that question. A map-driven platform that lets you find licensed physiotherapy clinics based on where you actually are, what insurance you have, and whether they're even taking patients — so that the next person with a broken limb doesn't have to travel an hour to fix it.

**What It Does**

📍 Locates physiotherapy clinics with pinpoint accuracy
🔍 Filters by distance, insurance coverage, and patient availability
🗺️ Map-driven interface built for speed and simplicity
📊 Proprietary location data verified manually for accuracy

**Tech Stack**
Frontend: React, JavaScript
Styling: Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
Icons: Lucide React
Routing: React Router
Maps: Leaflet.js

**Status**

Currently in active development. Major Canadian cities are being completed first, with smaller markets to follow. Public launch coming soon.
